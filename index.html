What an asset reconstruction company does

Banks and financial institutions carry loans that have stopped performing. An ARC takes those loans off their books and does the specialist work of recovering value from them, so lenders can return to lending.

Our business model
  1. 1 Acquisition. We acquire non-performing financial assets from banks and financial institutions, individually or as portfolios, funded through security receipts issued to qualified investors.
  2. 2 Resolution. Each account is worked towards resolution: restructuring the debt, a negotiated settlement, revival of the business, or enforcement of security under the SARFAESI Act and the insolvency code.
  3. 3 Realisation. Secured assets are sold through public e-auctions, and recoveries are passed back to the holders of security receipts.
