How to take part

Every e-auction follows the terms in its own sale notice and tender document. Read both before you bid; the steps below are the usual sequence.

Questions about an auction
  1. 1Read the notice. Download the sale notice and tender document for the asset and check the description, reserve price and dates.
  2. 2Inspect. Arrange an inspection with the authorised officer named in the notice, on the dates it sets.
  3. 3Register and deposit. Register on the e-auction platform named in the notice and pay the earnest money deposit before the deadline.
  4. 4Submit the tender. Send the signed tender form with KYC documents and proof of the deposit.
  5. 5Bid online. Bid on the auction date, in the increments the notice specifies.
  6. 6Pay and complete. The successful bidder pays the balance within the time set in the notice; the sale is then confirmed and a sale certificate issued.

Terms and disclaimers

Assets are sold on an as-is basis
Secured assets are sold on an “as is where is, as is what is and whatever there is” basis. Bidders should satisfy themselves about title, encumbrances and statutory dues before bidding.
The sale notice prevails
Where anything on this page differs from the published sale notice or tender document, the notice and tender document prevail.
Right to accept or reject bids
The authorised officer may accept or reject any bid, or postpone or cancel an auction, as set out in the terms of sale.
