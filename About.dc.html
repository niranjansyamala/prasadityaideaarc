Eighteen years of turning stressed loans back into value.

Prasaditya ARC Limited, formerly Pridhvi Asset Reconstruction and Securitisation Company Limited (PARAS), was the first asset reconstruction company licensed in South India. We buy non-performing loans from banks and financial institutions and work each one to the best available resolution.

Who we are

An asset reconstruction company exists so that lenders do not have to become specialists in recovery. We take non-performing loans off the books of banks and financial institutions, hold them in trusts funded by security receipts, and do the patient work of restructuring, settlement, revival or, where it is the only route, enforcement and sale.

We are headquartered in Hyderabad and regulated by the Reserve Bank of India. Our board and management are drawn largely from the recovery and legal functions of India's public-sector banks.

How our business works

Our purpose

Creating value through the revival and reconstruction of impaired assets.

How we work

Every account is worked towards the outcome that recovers the most, and every secured asset we sell goes through a public e-auction.

Who we answer to

Holders of security receipts, our shareholders, and the Reserve Bank of India, under the SARFAESI Act, 2002.
